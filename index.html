About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, advised by Dr. Guanpeng Li, and expect to graduate in Spring 2027. Before coming to UF, I earned my Master's degree in Computer Science (2022) and my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (2019) from the University of Iowa.

My research focuses on approximate computing, GPU lossy compression, HPC fault tolerance, and autonomous driving safety.
